# Simple Money Sheets

Free, private personal-finance calculators: everything runs in your browser, with no sign-up and nothing sent anywhere.
Tax numbers come from IRS and SSA primary sources, cited on each page.

**Website: https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/**

## Debt, loans, and budgeting
- [Debt snowball vs avalanche calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/debt-payoff-calculator/): debt-free date and total interest under both methods
- [Credit card payoff calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/credit-card-payoff-calculator/): minimum payments vs a fixed payment
- [Loan calculator with extra payments](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/loan-calculator/): full amortization schedule, extra monthly or lump-sum payments
- [Car affordability calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/car-affordability-calculator/): car price from a monthly budget, by loan term
- [Debt-to-income ratio calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/debt-to-income-ratio-calculator/): your DTI against common lender limits
- [50/30/20 budget calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/budget-calculator/)
- [Savings goal calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/savings-goal-calculator/): how long to reach a goal, or the monthly amount you need
- [Emergency fund calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/emergency-fund-calculator/): target, milestones, and how long savings would last

## Self-employed and freelance taxes (2026 and 2027)
- [Quarterly estimated tax calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/quarterly-estimated-tax-calculator/): Form 1040-ES worksheet and safe harbors
- [Self-employment tax calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/self-employment-tax-calculator/): Schedule SE, Social Security wage base
- [1099 vs W-2 calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/1099-vs-w2-calculator/): take-home pay and break-even contractor rate
- [Mileage deduction calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/mileage-deduction-calculator/): both 2026 IRS rates (72.5 and 76 cents a mile)
- [Home office deduction calculator](https://jesse-py.github.io/simple-money-sheets/home-office-deduction-calculator/): simplified vs regular method (Form 8829)

The tax engine is also open source: [Jesse-py/freelance-tax-estimator](https://github.com/Jesse-py/freelance-tax-estimator).
