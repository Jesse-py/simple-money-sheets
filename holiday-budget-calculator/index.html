How to set a holiday budget

Start with a list, not a total. Write down everyone you buy for and a dollar amount for each, add the costs that aren't gifts, and the total is your budget. Then subtract what you've already set aside and divide the rest by the paychecks you have left before you shop.

The example filled in above: 14 people on the gift list come to $560 (3 kids at $75, 4 adults at $50, 3 friends at $25, 4 others at $15), and food, decorations, cards, and other costs add $255, for a total of $815. With $200 already set aside, the remaining $615 over 3 paychecks is $205 per paycheck.

How much do people spend on the holidays?

In the National Retail Federation's 2025 holiday survey of 8,247 adults, people planned to spend $890.49 each on average: $627.93 on gifts for family and friends and $262.56 on seasonal items like food or candy, decorations, and greeting cards. That's the average per adult, not per household, and it's an average, not a target. NRF's 2026 survey, released in October 2026, didn't publish a per-person figure.

About 29% of that average went to things other than gifts. People who budget only for gifts are usually surprised by the rest, so the calculator asks for those costs separately.

Pay with cash you've set aside

A holiday budget is only as good as the money behind it. If part of it goes on a credit card you can't pay off in January, the interest makes every gift cost more. The credit card payoff calculator shows how long a balance takes to clear and what it costs. If you're short:

  • Trim the list before you trim the amounts. A name draw or a family gift exchange cuts the count, which saves more than shaving a few dollars off everyone.
  • Watch the non-gift costs. Hosting, travel, and decorations are easy to scale back and rarely missed.
  • Move the money on payday. Transfer each paycheck's amount to savings the day it arrives, so it isn't spent on something else first.

Start next year's fund in January

The holidays come on the same date every year, which makes them the easiest expense to save for in advance. Divide the total by 12 and set that much aside each month, starting in January, and December is already paid for. That's what a sinking fund is.

FAQ

How much should I spend per person on gifts?

There's no right number; it's whatever fits after your bills and savings. Work backward: decide the total you can pay in cash, take off the non-gift costs, and divide what's left by the people on your list.

How many paychecks do I have left before Christmas?

If you're paid every two weeks, count the paydays between today and the day you want to finish shopping. The calculator fills in an estimate from today's date (whole two-week periods until December 20); change it if your payday or deadline is different.

Should I include travel?

Yes, if you're traveling for the holidays. Flights, gas, and hotels can cost more than the gifts, and they're part of what the holidays cost you.
